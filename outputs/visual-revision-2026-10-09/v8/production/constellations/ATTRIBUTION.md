# Stellar Odyssey constellation derivatives

Six outline derivatives, dated 2026-10-09. Original illustrations: **Johan Meuris**, Stellarium constellation art. Vector tracing, simplified grayscale contours and three-star calibration: **Trần Vũ Anh Duy portfolio / Codex**.

These are modified derivatives of the publicly distributed raster illustrations. No original vector master or higher resolution master has been verified. Gemini reference is 256×256; the five other references are 512×512. Derivative SVGs contain real vector paths, no raster image payloads or AI-generated astronomy.
