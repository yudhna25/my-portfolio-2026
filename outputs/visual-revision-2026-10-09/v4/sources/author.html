Stellarium constellation art

I made the 88 Western constellation drawings for Stellarium, the free and open source planetarium software. Download and install the program for Windows, Linux or Mac to see the illustrations as they are meant to be seen. These textures were all made with Illustrator and Photoshop. Later on I added drawings from Inuit starlore. All images are released under the Free Art License.

I also occasionally help the project out with icon and GUI design.
